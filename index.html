Yiheng Xiong

I am a PhD student at DFG Research Training Group Knowledge Infusion and Extraction for Explainable Medical AI (KEMAI), Ulm University, working with Prof. Michael Götz. Previously, I obtained my Master's degree from the Technical University of Munich and my Bachelor's degree from Nanjing University.

My current research interests focus on improving the robustness of neural networks under domain shift, primarily through domain adaptation and domain generalization, with a particular emphasis on leveraging weak supervision.
